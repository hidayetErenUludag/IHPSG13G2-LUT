Every value comes from a DC operating-point sweep of the IHP-Open-PDK model: VGS in 20 mV steps (50 mV for HV), VDS in 50 mV steps (100 mV for HV), one single-finger device per L and finger width. Between grid points the page interpolates linearly along VGS; L, VDS and Wf are grid values.
Size by fingers, not by total width. In SG13G2 the LV devices change with finger width (LV NMOS Vth drops from 0.29 V at 1 µm to 0.21 V at 50 µm; LV PMOS leaves its valid range above 10 µm), while ng fingers of the same Wf scale exactly. Checked against direct simulation of the rounded device: ID, gm and gds within 3 %, fT within 8 % (the table is conservative for multi-finger devices).
Cgg includes gate overlap capacitance, so fT = gm/(2π·Cgg) matches PSP's own fug. Cdd adds the drain junction and gate-drain overlap. PMOS values are magnitudes. Full-precision tables (npz/lut_<device>_wf<Wf>.npz, including noise PSDs) and the generator gen_lut.py are in eda/designs/ihp_lut.
